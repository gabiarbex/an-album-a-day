privacy policy

Last updated: November 4, 2026

the short version

An Album a Day is a free music-discovery and listening-diary project. Guest mode works without an account. Most guest data stays in your browser. If you choose to sign in when cloud sync is available, selected app data can be saved to your account so it follows you across devices.

We do not sell your personal information or use it for targeted advertising.

what the app processes

Depending on how you use the site, the app may process:

  • your mood selections, free-text feeling prompt, energy, familiarity and listening-time preferences used to generate an album recommendation;
  • your listening diary, ratings, favorite tracks, notes, recommendation history and no-repeat history;
  • derived music-taste information, such as artist or tag weights created from information you choose to import;
  • basic account information supplied through Google sign-in, such as your email address and account identifier, if you choose to create an account;
  • ordinary technical information generated by hosting and authentication providers, such as IP address, browser information, timestamps and request logs.

guest mode and browser storage

Without an account, the app stores information such as your diary, albums already shown to you, recent artists, streak data and taste preferences in browser storage on your device. Clearing your browser's site data can remove this local information.

accounts and cloud sync

If you choose to sign in, An Album a Day may sync your diary, ratings, notes, no-repeat history, streak information and derived taste profile through Supabase. Existing guest data is designed to merge into your account rather than be replaced.

Your raw Last.fm CSV file is not uploaded as part of account sync. The app may store or sync only derived information created from that file, such as artist weights or music-neighbor data.

recommendation requests

Information you enter to request a recommendation, including free-text feeling descriptions and selected filters, may be sent to the project's recommendation backend so the app can rank albums for you. This information is used to provide the requested feature and is not used to sell advertising profiles.

service providers

The project relies on third-party infrastructure and services that may process limited information as necessary to provide the app. These currently include Google for optional OAuth sign-in, Supabase for authentication and account sync, Vercel for backend services, and GitHub Pages for public site hosting. Music metadata or discovery features may also use public music-data services.

Those providers operate under their own privacy policies and security practices.

retention and deletion

Guest information remains on your device until you delete it, clear site storage, or use an in-app removal control. Account data may remain in the cloud while your account exists. If account deletion is not yet available directly in the interface, you can request deletion through the project contact below.

children

An Album a Day is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children under the age required to consent to online services in their jurisdiction.

changes

This policy may change as the project develops. Material changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.
